Trained with

  • Angular loss: the exact geodesic angle, weighted half on all 20 steps and half on step 20.
  • Window sampling: every training window is equally likely, so small users are not re-drawn and memorised.
  • World tilt: half the windows are turned 0–15° about a random horizontal axis. Only the gravity channels change.
  • Native maps: training uses the same sin θ input and Cayley output as the graph, so the graph needs no series.
  • 40 epochs × 1,000 steps, batch 256, lr 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4, dropout 0.1, weight EMA 0.999.
